# EXECUTIVE DISORDER

> One decision. A thousand consequences.

**Executive Disorder** is a political satire and narrative management game where you play as the head of government of a fictional republic on the brink of collapse.

Every day brings new crises, morally questionable choices, fragile alliances, and unpredictable consequences. Your decisions shape public consensus, national finances, institutional stability, and social tension.

When everything seems lost, one final option remains: press the red button.

Once per run. Extraordinary power. Permanent consequences. An increasingly unstable world.

## Vision

Our goal is to create a satirical decision-making game where every choice has a cost, consequences can emerge days later, and the player never has complete control over the situation.

The game combines:

- **Desk Management:** An interactive government workstation inspired by diegetic management games.
- **Daily Decisions:** A day-by-day gameplay loop built around crises, priorities, and difficult choices.
- **Emergent Narrative:** Branching events and cascading consequences driven by previous decisions.
- **Political Satire:** Bureaucracy, propaganda, political compromises, and institutional absurdity.
- **The Red Button:** A one-time emergency mechanic that fundamentally changes the rest of the run.
- **Multiple Endings:** Different outcomes determined by the state of the country and the player's decisions.

## Core Gameplay Loop

1. **Read:** Review documents, intelligence reports, news, and requests from ministers.
2. **Decide:** Choose how to handle the current crisis.
3. **Manage:** Allocate resources and maintain the political balance.
4. **Face the Consequences:** Deal with immediate reactions and delayed events.
5. **Survive:** Keep the government running until the next day.
6. **Escalate:** If necessary, press the red button and deal with the fallout.

## Core Systems

- Government resources: Consensus, Treasury, Stability, and Tension.
- Daily event and decision system.
- Conditional and random events.
- Persistent consequences and event chains.
- One-time emergency Red Button mechanic.
- Narrative state tracking and ending conditions.
- Interactive government desk and diegetic UI.

## Visual Direction

A retro government office filled with warm cinematic lighting, bureaucratic props, vintage monitors, paper documents, emergency telephones, and a prominent red button.

The interface should feel like a physical government workspace rather than a conventional menu-driven strategy game.

Our visual references combine bureaucratic realism, political caricature, retro aesthetics, and increasingly absurd situations.

## Tech Stack

- **Engine:** Unreal Engine 5
- **Language:** C++
- **UI:** Unreal Motion Graphics (UMG), with Common UI to be evaluated
- **Version Control:** Git and GitHub
- **Game Content:** Data Assets or Data Tables for configurable events and decisions, to be evaluated during prototyping

## Project Status

**Current Phase:** Pre-production

We are defining the core gameplay loop, narrative structure, visual identity, and technical architecture before committing to a larger production scope.

Our first milestone is a small playable prototype featuring an interactive government desk, a handful of decisions, four core resources, and an initial implementation of the Red Button mechanic.

## Development Principles

- Prototype before expanding scope.
- Prefer data-driven events over hardcoded narrative logic.
- Make major decisions produce meaningful consequences.
- Keep the project achievable for a two-person indie team.
- Document important design decisions and rejected alternatives.
- Prioritize a compelling gameplay loop over feature count.
